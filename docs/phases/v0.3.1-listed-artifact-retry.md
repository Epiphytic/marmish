No internal request loop, new grace, budget reset or workflow rerun. Persistent 404 ends at the first deadline/budget boundary; transient 404 retries are quiet and terminal diagnostics describe deadline/budget rather than retaining the earlier 404. Existing body-cancel waits receive no new independent deadline.
