## r1 blockers
- **B1 (recovered Status didn't check output): fixed.** After reopen and before the gate, `recovered_prefix` (`command_check.rs:956`) requires:
  - `running_eof`
  - the exact `before-restart` / `before-stderr` prefixes
  - the server's produced counts equal to the verified lengths (14 and 13)
  - no gaps on either stream
  - both streams still open
- **B2 (report contract): fixed.**
  - `recovery_report()` (`:947`) starts every field closed: false or null, with `failed_stage` updated stage by stage.
  - `purpose_ownership_unknown` is now observed through `journal.publication_owned` and `command_publication_owned`, and must be true or the run fails.
  - Fanouts now come from `storage.list_outbound_fanouts()`.
  - The stop, open and activation timings are recorded separately.
- **B3 (EngineTree extraction): fixed.**
  - The design explicitly allows a `pub(super)` type with private fields, a "narrowly scoped capture_path", a private shared removal body, and `remove_installed(self, &mut OwnedCleanupGuard)`.
  - `remove_installed` (`:1519`) runs in this order:
    1. await `guard.revalidate()`;
    2. a synchronous `verify_path(guard.profile())`, which compares the original parent and root inodes and is never a recapture;
    3. a synchronous `remove_inner`, with no await between it and step 2;
    4. `after_unlink()` on the same guard.
  - The guard's `profile` field is private and set once, from `prepare`. `profile()` returns `&Path`, and nothing mutates it.
  - The guard is `pub(in crate::mdk_adapter)` and borrows the live `Child`.
  - The plain `remove()` stays private.
  - This closes the r1 gap where a guard could authorise deleting a tree captured from a different path.

## Non-blockers
1. **Approval isn't recorded in the repo.** The installed-cleanup design and its r3 approval exist only under `/tmp`. Also, `docs/phases/v0.2-phase2-command-recovery-check.md:310-313` still says "No capture_path or remove visibility expansion is authorized here." Commit the design and approval into `docs/` and point the phase doc at them, so the authorisation doesn't rest on scratch files.
2. **`capture_path` isn't feature-gated.** It's `pub(super)` in all builds; only `remove_installed` is behind the feature. This is harmless because a receipt grants no deletion, but gating it with `#[cfg(feature="installed-checks")]` or `cfg_attr` would narrow it further.
3. **Prefix bytes aren't re-read after reopen.** The check relies on the server's produced counts, the absence of gaps and cursor continuity. The final poll continues from cursors 14/13 and must yield exactly `after-restart` / `after-stderr`. Re-reading from cursor 0 after reopen would be a stronger byte-level check, but what's there is sufficient.
4. **Feature-enabled checks are outside this review.** `cargo test` and `cargo clippy -D warnings` with `--features installed-checks` belong to the sibling's installed workflow gate. Nothing in the r2 logs shows them.
5. **Carried over from r1, still open:**
   - `PendingStop::finish` ignores its result; this is safe because cleanup is retained.
   - The pinned `server_open` future is awaited without a time limit; the design leaves the bound to the external supervisor.
   - The probe's 15 s clock starts when the probe is called, not when it's sent.
