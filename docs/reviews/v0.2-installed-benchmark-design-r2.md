Its resolve job mints the run's common `context.json` before the role jobs queue, with lifetime of at most 3300s. Keep the existing no-renewal and no-remint sentences.

## Nonblockers

- **Rename `room_prepare_busy` to `local_busy`.** The CLI cannot tell a Prepare/room-open busy apart from a non-ambiguous Submit busy. Both print exactly `marmish: local_busy` and exit 255 (`command_cli.rs:425-437` and `:1104`). A 255 exit with no handle line should also fall under the unknown-admission rule: preserve the tree and do not cancel, even though the product means it as non-admitted.
- **Make the reservation match the checks.** Line 115 says "600 conservative preparation reservation", but the two checks leave 450s between prepare entry (2670) and cohort entry (2220). Say that prepare plus supervisor handoff must fit in 450s, or the cohort check fails closed.
- **State the redispatch rule.** Local-workflows lines 178-179 let a same-candidate redispatch recover only closed pre-spawn infrastructure failures (queue-late, entry-budget, asset download). Say that a prepare or cohort entry-budget failure before any product spawn falls in that class. Any failure after a daemon spawn needs a fresh identified cohort, with the earlier evidence kept.

Once blocker 1 is fixed, I expect the design to be approvable. Implementation still needs the common local-workflows design approved, the autoexit and shared cohort-table wording reconciled, and the coordinated Context, prepare and cleanup row-bound changes integrated after the matrix freeze.
