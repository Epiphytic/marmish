## What I checked against the approved design
- **Matches the design:**
  - The sequence runs prepare, then cohort, sequentially under one shared deadline, and a failed prepare never starts the cohort.
  - The first-step check passes: `expires_at + 360s <= jobDeadline`, with the bound taken as `min(expiry, jobDeadline - 300s)`.
  - The child env is only PATH, HOME, TMPDIR and LANG, with no Actions token.
  - The child is detached and signalled through the negative-pid group, TERM then KILL, and stdout/stderr are drained concurrently, still draining after the cap.
  - A pre-aborted signal is never green, and abort between the two steps stops the sequence.
  - A missing executable fails in 5ms, so `close` fires on a spawn error.
  - A `setsid` escapee holding the pipe is bounded at deadline+grace+close (902ms in the probe) and rejects.
  - The six-file collector is strict: it rejects extras, symlinks and a role directory that is not a plain directory.
- **`workflow.mjs`/`workflow.test.mjs` delta (`cohortLifetime`, `verifyContext`):** approved. `__proto__`, `constructor` and the empty string are rejected, and `verifyContext` rejects an input/Context cohort mismatch.

## Required changes
1. **Group KILL depends on the leader still being open (`local.mjs:21`, `:58-63`).** In my probe the leader died on TERM, `close` fired, and the escalation timer was cleared. A TERM-ignoring grandchild stayed alive in the same group and was never KILLed. The design says to TERM the owned group, allow at most 60s, then KILL it. Fix: always SIGKILL the group in `finally`, and drop the `closed` short-circuit. Use `kill(-pid, 0)` to see whether members remain. Do not add a delayed timer after close, because of pid reuse.
2. **A clean leader exit passes even if descendants survive (`local.mjs:46`).** In my probe the leader exited 0 and `sleep` stayed alive in the group, yet `runOwned` resolved in 3ms. So prepare residue can run into the cohort, which breaks the one-owned-child-at-a-time rule. Fix: after `close`, probe the group. If any member remains, SIGKILL it and reject with `local_group_residue`.
3. **Missing tests.** Add:
   - residue after exit 0;
   - a TERM-ignoring survivor after the leader dies;
   - a grandchild that holds the pipe after the leader exits (already passes, 401ms; pin it);
   - the `setsid` escapee (already passes; pin it);
   - a mid-run abort that latches failure and TERMs then KILLs the group;
   - credential exclusion (`GITHUB_TOKEN`, `ACTIONS_RUNTIME_TOKEN`, `ACTIONS_ID_TOKEN_REQUEST_TOKEN`) in the child env;
   - the missing executable under production defaults;
   - collector cases for a missing role directory, a symlinked role directory, an existing destination and an oversize record.

## Should fix, non-blocking
- **Signal handlers (`local.mjs:122`).** `process.once` drops the handler after the first signal, and SIGHUP is not handled. A second SIGTERM or SIGINT during the grace window kills the supervisor by default action and skips the group KILL. Use `on`, SIGKILL the group on a repeated signal, and handle SIGHUP. The exit is still nonzero, so it is never green.
- **Resolve wiring (`workflow.mjs:11`).** No test shows that `resolve` passes the cohort and `now + table` seconds to the driver, and `verifyContext` checks cohort equality only. This matches the design text. Optionally check `expires_at - created_at` against the table.
