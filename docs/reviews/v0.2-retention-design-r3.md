## Remaining blocker

**B3 — Anyone outside the allow-list can use the new `admission_reject_through` frontier to keep all new rooms rejected indefinitely.**
- Every validation failure in `project_join` writes a `rejected_groups` row, including `untrusted_peer` (`mdk_adapter.rs:1245`, `:667-668`). The doc doesn't say which rejections use a row.
- Our KeyPackage is public, so anyone can send us Welcomes, and MDK joins them automatically, producing `GroupJoined`.
- An attacker can send about 4096 Welcomes in seven days to fill the rejection table. After that, one Welcome every few minutes with a session timestamp of about now moves the frontier up to now each time.
- The result is that every legitimate new admission, and our own room creation, stays in `admission_backpressure`. The amendment made overflow finite per event, but this makes it permanent from the outside.
- **Fix:** policy rejections (untrusted peer, purpose, membership, missing author, malformed or invalid session) are deterministic. They are forgotten and acknowledged without a durable row, and they never move the frontier. Re-checking them always gives the same answer, and replays still face the freshness check.
  - Only allowed peers that pass policy and are then refused for capacity or metadata reasons may write a row or move the frontier.
  - That means checking policy before capacity, so only authorized peers can push the frontier.
  - Add a test: a flood of untrusted Welcomes cannot change `admission_reject_through` or the row count.

## B1 and B2 confirmed fixed
- **B1:** IDs more than five minutes in the future are deferred and left unacknowledged, including during `clock_behind_floor`. Only values beyond `max(now, floor) + 7d` are refused permanently, and future IDs cannot move the frontier.
- **B2:** a native group with no journal decision and no pending join is now treated as an orphan. It is reconciled on open and after a local create failure, and cleanup is proven by forgetting it and re-listing pending events. That covers the gap between `create_group` and `accept_conversation` at `mdk_adapter.rs:995-1004`, including the non-crash failure paths.

## Implementation notes (not blocking)
1. **Orphan enumeration needs more than `live_group_ids`.** In the engine (`engine.rs:1815`), `live_group_ids()` only returns groups that have in-memory epoch state, so unhydrated and quarantined groups are left out. Reconcile against the union of `live_group_ids`, `unhydrated_group_ids`, `quarantined_groups` and `outbound_fanout_group_ids`, or hydrate first. Otherwise an orphan can still go unnoticed.
2. **The doc's statements match the APIs.** The upstream pending outbox only accepts `MessageReceived` and `GroupJoined`, keyed by `message_id`, so the round-robin key covers every entry. `delete_pending_application_events` ignores unknown IDs and `forget_group_local` returns `Ok(false)` when repeated, so cleanup is idempotent. The three native-obligation sources all exist as named.
3. **Frontier timing is bounded.** An accepted session timestamp is at most now plus five minutes, so after a legitimate capacity refusal the collateral block on our own room creation lasts about five minutes at most.
